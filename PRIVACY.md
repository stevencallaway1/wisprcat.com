# Built for absolute privacy

Most AI dictation apps send a recording of your voice to the cloud, and through multiple service providers for processing. Every transfer creates another vulnerability where it could be stored, accessed, or exposed.

Wisprcat removes that risk. It runs entirely on your Mac, so your audio never leaves your device. Not "deleted after processing." Never uploaded in the first place.

## The one network request

Wisprcat makes exactly one network request in its entire lifecycle: downloading its speech model, once, on first launch. After that finishes, you can turn off Wi-Fi and Wisprcat works exactly the same.

**Try it yourself.** After first launch, disconnect from Wi-Fi and dictate normally. If Wisprcat needed a server, this is where it would fail. It won't.

## What Wisprcat does

- **Runs locally on Apple Silicon.** No external services, no round trip.
- **No cloud dependency. No API key.** Speech is processed on your device. After the model downloads, dictation works completely offline.
- **Explicit permissions.** Microphone access is required, and nothing more.
- **No telemetry.** Your usage is never tracked.
- **No audio leaves your machine.** Private by default, not by setting.

## What we're honest about

Wisprcat doesn't carry formal compliance certifications like SOC 2, ISO 27001, or a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement. If your organization needs one of those for procurement, that's a real constraint, and we'd rather say so than dress up an architectural privacy story as something it isn't. There simply isn't a server for that certification to be about.
