# What's new in Wisprcat

Dated highlights from active development.

## August 2026: Smart Dictation

The biggest release yet. On-device AI cleanup that removes filler words, fixes punctuation and capitalization, and resolves obvious self-corrections, entirely on your Mac. Also: fixed microphone selection silently following the wrong input device, and hardened against silent crashes and permission loops.

## July 2026: Custom vocabulary

Added a personal dictionary. Teach Wisprcat your names, products, and jargon, and it biases recognition toward those terms and applies literal replacements. Also: live checkout wired directly into the site.

## April 2026: First release

Wisprcat ships. Push-to-talk dictation: hold your shortcut, speak, release, text appears wherever you're typing. Menu bar only, no Dock icon, no account, no subscription.
