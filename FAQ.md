# Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you buy.

## Does my audio ever leave my Mac?

No. Wisprcat records only while you hold the hotkey, transcribes locally on Apple Silicon, and discards the audio afterward. There are no accounts, no API keys, and no telemetry. The only network request the app makes is a one-time download of the open-source speech model on first launch.

## Will this actually work for my workflow?

Yes. Wisprcat works in every text field: Gmail, Slack, Notion, VS Code, ChatGPT, and everything else. One hotkey, every app.

## How much time will I save?

Most users save 20+ hours per month. If you type two to four hours a day, Wisprcat cuts that to under an hour, giving you back 1 to 3 hours every day.

## Is Wisprcat a subscription?

No. Wisprcat is $39, paid once. You own the version you buy, permanently. No account is created and there is nothing to cancel.

## Can I use it on multiple Macs?

Your license is per person. Install Wisprcat on every Mac you personally use.

## Does it work without the internet?

Yes, after setup. Wisprcat downloads its speech model during installation or first launch. Transcription then runs locally on your Mac from that point on.

## What makes Wisprcat different?

Wisprcat gives you fast dictation with privacy-first design. Most other voice dictation tools send your audio to the cloud to transcribe it. Wisprcat runs entirely on your Mac, so your words never leave your machine.

## Why does macOS show a warning when I first open it?

Current builds aren't Apple-notarized yet, so the first launch takes one approval in System Settings, Privacy & Security, then Open Anyway. It takes under a minute and happens once.

## What permissions does it need?

Microphone, so it can hear you while the hotkey is held. Accessibility is optional and only needed for automatic paste. Without it, Wisprcat leaves the transcript on your clipboard.

## Which operating systems are supported?

macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later, on Apple Silicon (M1 or newer). Wisprcat does not run on Intel Macs.

## What languages does it support?

English, currently. If you need another language today, Wisprcat isn't the right fit yet.

## How accurate is the transcription?

Wisprcat runs a best-in-class open-source speech engine, tuned for real-time dictation. It handles natural speech, accents, and technical vocabulary well. You can always edit the text after it lands.
