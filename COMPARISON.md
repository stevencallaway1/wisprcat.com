## Why the price gap exists

Cloud dictation tools pay a real per-minute server cost every time you speak, so a recurring charge covers ongoing compute. Wisprcat's cleanup and transcription both run on hardware you already own. No server meter, nothing to charge you for every month.

That's the whole reason a $39 app and a $15/month subscription sit in the same category. Different architectures, not just different pricing.

## The privacy architecture, compared honestly

This isn't a claim that cloud competitors are careless with your data. Their privacy pages generally say the right things, and some carry real enterprise certifications Wisprcat doesn't have. The difference is structural, not reputational.

| | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Local processing** (Wisprcat) | Audio never leaves the device. No server in the loop to trust, secure, or subpoena. |
| **Cloud, zero-retention promise** (most competitors' default) | Audio leaves the device, gets processed elsewhere, and is promised to be discarded afterward. |
| **Cloud, privacy-mode toggle** (some competitors) | Retention depends on a setting you or an admin controls, and can be changed. |

Wisprcat only has the first mode. There's no toggle, because there's no upload path to toggle.

**What Wisprcat doesn't have:** enterprise compliance certifications (SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA BAA), team or admin controls, multi-seat billing, cross-device sync. If your organization needs those, a cloud competitor with the right certification is the better fit. That's a real tradeoff, not a gap we're hiding.

## vs. Apple Dictation (built in, free)

Apple Dictation is free and, for short bursts on newer Macs, genuinely decent. Three things matter if you dictate for a living:

- **Interaction.** Wisprcat is push-to-talk. Hold, speak, release, done. No session to start, no timeout to manage.
- **Vocabulary.** Custom vocabulary handles names, products, and jargon you use daily, with decode-time biasing and literal replacement, not just a generic dictionary.
- **Control.** One hotkey, no cloud fallback mode at any setting to accidentally leave on.

If Apple Dictation already works for you, keep using it. Wisprcat is for the people it doesn't work for.

## The honest summary

Want multilingual support, team collaboration, cross-device sync, or compliance certifications? A cloud subscription tool is the better product for you, genuinely.

Want your voice to stay on your own machine, and would rather pay once than rent a keyboard forever? That's Wisprcat.
